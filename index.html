The Analyst Files · Case #01
THE CRASHING DASHBOARD
Food Delivery

Setup

You are a Product Analyst at a fast-growing food-delivery app. It is 9:40 on a Monday and three Slack messages are already waiting: the PM wants to know if Friday's new feature “worked,” Ops is puzzled by a 3-minute jump in delivery ETA, and Growth is alarmed by a 5% overnight drop in cart conversion.

Evidence

  • Three stakeholders, three completely different questions — all landed at once.
  • Not one of them is a query you can just run. The data exists; the thinking doesn't.
  • You have the exact same tools as every analyst on the team. So does everyone else.

The Mystery

Which question do you tackle first — and what is your very first move before you touch any data?

Section 3

Prerequisites to Refresh

Make sure these from the Pandas module are fresh — today's frameworks sit on top of your data-manipulation baseline.

DataFrame operations
You'll reason about data grouped by user, time, or feature. Being able to mentally model a groupby or filter helps you follow the diagnostic examples.
Aggregations (count, sum, mean)
Every metric today — conversion rate, ETA, retention — is an aggregation. Knowing what “average delivery time” actually computes keeps the discussion grounded.
Filtering & conditional logic
CRIED's “Rule Out” step mentally slices data (only iOS? only one city?). Practice slicing a DataFrame by one or two conditions.
Time-series basics
Metrics move over time. Comfort with weekly vs. daily rollups helps you tell a real trend from a windowing artifact.
